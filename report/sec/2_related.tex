\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{6D Pose Estimation}
6D pose estimation has been extensively studied in computer vision. Early methods relied on feature matching and template-based approaches. PoseCNN~\cite{xiang2018posecnn} pioneered end-to-end deep learning for pose estimation, introducing the ADD metric that we use for evaluation. The ADD metric measures the average distance between 3D model points transformed by predicted and ground truth poses.

\subsection{Rotation Representations}
Representing 3D rotations for neural networks is non-trivial. Common representations include Euler angles, rotation matrices, and quaternions~\cite{shoemake1985animating}. We use unit quaternions due to their continuous nature and freedom from gimbal lock.

\subsection{Loss Landscape Analysis}
Understanding the optimization landscape is crucial for training deep networks. Li \etal~\cite{visualloss} proposed filter-normalized random directions for visualizing loss landscapes, revealing that network architecture affects landscape smoothness. We adopt their methodology to analyze our pose estimation networks.
