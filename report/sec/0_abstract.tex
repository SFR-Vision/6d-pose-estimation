\begin{abstract}
Accurate 6D object pose estimation is crucial for robotic manipulation, augmented reality, and autonomous systems. This work presents a geometric-aware deep learning approach for estimating the 6D pose (3D rotation and 3D translation) of objects from RGB images. We propose an RGB-Geometric model that incorporates camera intrinsics and bounding box geometry to improve translation prediction, particularly the depth (Z) component. Our approach uses a ResNet-18 backbone with specialized heads for rotation (quaternion) and translation prediction. We evaluate our method on the LineMOD dataset, achieving 33\% ADD-2cm accuracy. Additionally, we extend our approach to RGB-D inputs as a bonus task, demonstrating the framework's flexibility. We analyze the loss landscape using filter-normalized random directions following Li \etal~\cite{visualloss}, providing insights into the optimization dynamics. Our results show that geometric priors significantly improve pose estimation accuracy compared to purely appearance-based methods.
\end{abstract}
