\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a geometric-aware approach for 6D object pose estimation that incorporates camera intrinsics and detection geometry to improve translation prediction. Our RGB-Geometric model achieves 33\% ADD-2cm accuracy on LineMOD by predicting only depth and deriving X, Y coordinates geometrically.

Key findings include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Geometric constraints significantly improve pose estimation (+8\% over baseline).
    \item Data augmentation provides limited benefit on controlled datasets like LineMOD.
    \item Loss landscape analysis shows smooth optimization with flat minima.
    \item Depth (Z) estimation remains the primary challenge in monocular pose estimation.
\end{itemize}

As a bonus task, we extended our approach to RGB-D inputs, achieving 38.5\% ADD-2cm accuracy, demonstrating the framework's flexibility.

\textbf{Future Work.} Potential improvements include iterative refinement, attention mechanisms, and training on larger datasets with more diverse conditions.
